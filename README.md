🔋 BEV & PHEV Vehicle Analysis | Power BI
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Battery Electric Vehicles (BEV) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEV) to understand adoption trends, regional distribution, manufacturer performance, and policy eligibility insights.

The dashboard provides a comparative analysis of BEV and PHEV vehicles, enabling stakeholders to evaluate market penetration, growth patterns, and strategic opportunities across different dimensions.

🎯 Objective:
To deliver data-driven insights that support decision-making in the electric vehicle ecosystem by analyzing vehicle types, states, manufacturers, and eligibility criteria.

📊 Data Source

Public Electric Vehicle Registration Dataset

The dataset consists of multiple structured tables including vehicle details, registration year, state-wise distribution, manufacturer information, and eligibility classification.

🛠 Tool

Power BI –

📈 Dashboard Pages
🔹 Page 1: BEV Vehicle Analysis

This page provides insights into Battery Electric Vehicles adoption and performance.

Key Highlights:

Total BEV vehicles and average driving range

Year-wise BEV growth trends

State-wise BEV distribution across the U.S.

Top manufacturers by BEV sales (Tesla, Nissan, Chevrolet, etc.)

CAFV (Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle) eligibility breakdown

🔹 Page 2: PHEV Vehicle Analysis

This page focuses on Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles, highlighting differences compared to BEVs.

Key Highlights:

Total PHEV vehicles and market share

Year-wise PHEV sales trends

State-level PHEV adoption analysis

Leading manufacturers (Toyota, Chevrolet, Ford, BMW, etc.)

CAFV eligibility insights for PHEV vehicles

🔍 Key Insights

BEVs dominate the EV market, accounting for the majority of electric vehicle registrations.

PHEVs contribute a smaller but significant share, offering flexibility through hybrid technology.

Certain states show strong EV adoption, while others indicate potential growth opportunities.

Manufacturer performance varies significantly between BEV and PHEV categories.

CAFV eligibility analysis helps assess policy impact on vehicle adoption.

🏁 Conclusion

This analysis highlights the current landscape and growth potential of BEV and PHEV vehicles.
The dashboards enable stakeholders to compare vehicle categories, identify high-performing regions and manufacturers, and evaluate eligibility-driven adoption patterns.

The insights derived from this project can support strategic planning, policy evaluation, and market expansion decisions in the electric vehicle industry.
